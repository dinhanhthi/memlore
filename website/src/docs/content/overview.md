# Overview

Memlore is a private journal on your device, locked with your password in an encrypted database.

:::diagram overview

## What stays on this device

- No Memlore account or server; we cannot read your journal.
- Writing and search work offline.
- Photos, video, and files stay here, but are not encrypted.
- On a Mac, Touch ID is an extra unlock, not a password replacement.
- Memlore cannot reset a lost password.
